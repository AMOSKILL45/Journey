recommendations to help you plan. This information is **indicative only**, may be incomplete, may not apply
to your specific nationality or circumstances, and can become **out of date** — entry rules change frequently
and without notice. **It is not legal, immigration, or travel advice.** You remain **solely responsible for
verifying all applicable requirements with the relevant official government authorities** before booking and
before travelling. [Company] makes no warranty as to the accuracy, completeness, or currency of this
information and accepts no liability for any loss or inconvenience arising from reliance on it. Some entries
display community-contributed reports (e.g. "flagged as outdated"); these are **unverified signals from other
users** and do not represent [Company]'s position.

ne représentent pas la position de [Société].
